# Code of Conduct

Kithairon contributors are expected to keep project discussions direct, respectful, and focused on improving the software and documentation.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific when reporting bugs, reviewing code, or disagreeing with a design.
- Give contributors enough context to reproduce problems.
- Separate technical criticism from personal criticism.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about project scope, release timing, and support boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private contact information or security details without consent.
- Repeated off-topic comments after maintainers ask for a thread to stop.
- Bad-faith reports, spam, or attempts to disrupt project infrastructure.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close issues, restrict participation, or block accounts when behavior harms the project or its contributors. To report a conduct issue, contact `gnuicecream@gmail.com` with relevant links or screenshots.
